# HYPERFRAMES — SUEPERMAN: THE HERO YOU DIDN'T ASK FOR

## THE MISSION

Create an extraordinary, cinematic, funny, emotionally engaging, 90-second animated product commercial for **SUEPERMAN — Your Consumer-Rights Agent**.

This commercial is being produced for a competitive AI hackathon. The objective is to create an unforgettable, award-caliber commercial that could plausibly run during the Super Bowl.

The entire commercial should resemble a premium, theatrically animated superhero short film, with a polished product demonstration seamlessly integrated into the story.

**Only cinematic and visual references:**

- **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)** — the primary animation reference: comic-book textures, expressive characters, kinetic typography, dynamic framing, and stylized motion.
- **Superman / DC universe** — the narrative world: Metropolis, Lois Lane, LexCorp, Superman's heroic grandeur, dramatic entrances, and superhero mythology.

Do not use references to any other movies, television series, franchises, advertisements, or pop-culture properties.

Make the commercial feel like something produced by a talented animation studio rather than a generic AI-generated promotional video.

## CORE CREATIVE PREMISE

Lois Lane's expensive LexCorp washing machine malfunctions in a maddeningly ordinary way.

A superhero appears.

Unfortunately, he's not Superman.

He's Sueperman.

He can't fix her washing machine.

But he can help determine whether her purchase qualifies for an official recall remedy and prepare the necessary paperwork.

The comedy comes from treating an extremely mundane consumer-rights workflow with the same dramatic seriousness as saving the planet.

**Product truth:** Sueperman is a website that discovers official product recalls and verified class-action settlements using purchase records, verifies relevant eligibility conditions, and prepares evidence-backed remedy packets for explicit user approval.

It is NOT a law firm, an appliance repair service, an automatic refund processor, or a system that files legal claims without user approval.

# COMEDY AND ACTING DIRECTION

The humor must feel intentional and character-driven.

Sueperman is never knowingly funny.

He is completely convinced he is a legitimate superhero performing essential heroic work.

Lois is the audience surrogate.

Her reactions should be expressive, believable, and perfectly timed.

The central comedic contrast is:

A SUPERMAN-SIZED ENTRANCE.

A WEBSITE-SIZED SUPERPOWER.

The washing machine should be frustrating because it is smug, expensive, and spectacularly incompetent—not because it causes property destruction.

The film must never mock people who experience unsafe products or miss compensation opportunities. The humor targets corporate bureaucracy, superhero theatrics, and Sueperman's exaggerated seriousness.

Do not add random jokes or unnecessary dialogue.

The three most important comedic moments are:

1. "Mostly keeping receipts."
2. Sueperman's overly heroic entrance to discuss consumer remedies.
3. "But I know how to get the paperwork started."

Give all three room to land.
